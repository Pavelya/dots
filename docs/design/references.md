# References

Screens from well-made mobile puzzle and board games, and photos of the paper game. They show
what "good" looks like for Pinfold's board, world map, capture moments, level cards and results.
Each entry links its source and says what to learn from it.

The image files are kept privately by the owner, outside the repository. The rules are in
[`docs/assets.md`](../assets.md): screenshots of other games never enter the repository and are
never uploaded to an image or music generator. Nothing here may be copied into Pinfold's art.

Most of these games play differently from Dots. The notes take one lesson from each screen, not
their rules.

## Board

### B01 BadukPop: intersection placement and last-move marker

[Source page](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/badukpop-go/id1472684271) ·
[Image](https://is1-ssl.mzstatic.com/image/thumb/Purple116/v4/21/d8/0c/21d80cf7-cf23-37cc-ab7c-139b17c8e4c0/6141c925-230c-4301-9d43-30d091a08d64_5.5-en-19x19-c.png/1290x2796bb.png) ·
iOS App Store screenshot

**Shows:** stones sit on line crossings, and the last white stone has a small dot in its centre.
Each player's name, score and clock sit above and below the board.

**For Pinfold:** mark the last dot placed. Keep turn, score and buttons off the grid.

### B02 Mini Metro: light board with clear line weights

[Source page](https://minireview.io/simulation/mini-metro) ·
[Image](https://minireview.io/common/uploads/review/e88da6978c82a1adc47897ef1dbded2e.jpg) ·
Android screenshot on MiniReview

**Shows:** coloured routes stand out from the pale river. Station shapes carry meaning without
labels.

**For Pinfold:** give the grid, the dots and the capture outlines three clearly different weights.
Give each player a second cue besides colour (a shape), for colour-blind players.

### B03 Mini Metro: crowded board on a dark background

[Source page](https://minireview.io/simulation/mini-metro) ·
[Image](https://minireview.io/common/uploads/review/26d752453b1253d93f55bf165b3bc492.jpg) ·
Android screenshot on MiniReview

**Shows:** a crowded network stays readable with thin lines, hollow station symbols and a small
toolbar at the edge.

**For Pinfold:** test a late-game 15x15 board at phone size. Keep captured areas pale enough that
every dot stays visible.

### B04 KAMI 2: paper surface with quiet controls

[Source page](https://www.stateofplaygames.com/kami2press) ·
[Image](https://images.squarespace-cdn.com/content/v1/57b5d1f0d482e93404415213/1491234931371-40KNF5JHZV2SJN1V7GAF/Kami2_InMotion4.PNG) ·
iPhone press screenshot from the developer

**Shows:** paper texture makes the screen warm. One narrow bottom bar holds the move count, undo,
hint and colours.

**For Pinfold:** the squared-paper look can carry texture, as long as it stays weaker than the
dots and outlines. Put boosters and undo in one slim bar.

### B05 Flow Free: end points against connecting paths

[Source page](https://www.bigduckgames.com/flowfree) ·
[Image](https://images.squarespace-cdn.com/content/v1/586beec5e58c624be9f7b5a2/1483734815212-BEB0UXSN3TQGK6PQWZTB/image-asset.png) ·
Screenshot from the developer

**Shows:** big round end points, thick paths and a thin square grid each have a clear role.

**For Pinfold:** a dot must look different from the line that joins it into a capture ring.

### B07 The Battle of Polytopia: territory borders on a crowded board

[Source page](https://minireview.io/strategy/the-battle-of-polytopia) ·
[Image](https://minireview.io/common/uploads/review/281816f785f78d8a524b1ccebd5fe19c.jpg) ·

**Shows:** coloured borders split the territories. Cities and units stay visible inside them.

**For Pinfold:** the capture outline matters more than the fill. Enemy dots inside a captured

### B09 SmartGo One: numbered last moves on a 13x13 board

[Source page](https://www.smartgo.com/screenshots.html) ·
[Image](https://www.smartgo.com/images/en-3-play-go-1242x2208.png) ·

**Shows:** a crowded 13x13 Go board on a phone. The last two stones carry their move numbers, and

**For Pinfold:** a board close to 15x15 can stay readable on a phone. Marking the last move of
each player helps someone who looked away follow the game.

### B10 SmartGo One: stones marked in a capture puzzle

### B11 Threes!: a tutorial that starts on the board

[Source page](https://www.businessinsider.com/threes-iphone-game-2014-3) ·
