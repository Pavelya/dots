# 0036: English only

**Date:** 2026-11-23 · **Status:** accepted · **Replaces:** [0007](0007-languages.md)

## Context

Decision 0007 planned English first, then Russian, Ukrainian and Polish, each translation checked
by a fluent speaker (plan task A-10). On 2026-11-22 the owner chose to ship in English only and
closed A-10 (Languages).

## Decision

- Pinfold ships in English only, at launch and after.
- The store listing is in English.

## Consequences

- Text stays in resource files, as it is now, so nothing in the app changes.
- Settings gets no Language row.
- Players in Russia, Ukraine and Poland, where the paper game is best known, play in English.
- Decisions 0007 and 0030 stay as written. Decision 0030's reason for making every cosmetic
  earnable still holds: players in Russia play in English and can't pay through Google Play.
