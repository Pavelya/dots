# 0005: Audience: casual adults, playful tone

**Date:** 2026-11-05 · **Status:** accepted

## Context

The look, the characters and the store rating depend on who the game is for. Background: the design investigation (https://claude.ai/artifact/DyiSkrrx3hwARfRpyxNGRP).

## Decision

- The game is made for casual adults.
- The tone is playful but not childish.

## Consequences

- The store audience is teens and adults, not "Designed for Families" (plan task R-04).
- Characters stay simple and appear around the board, not as the pieces.
