# 0020: Few sound effects; three of them from Suno Sounds

**Date:** 2026-11-08 · **Status:** accepted

## Context

Decision 0013 says sound effects are recorded or generated by us, plus CC0 sounds, and the first
sound list had 38 effects, 21 of them recorded with a phone. Many players keep the sound off, and
sounds inside a level easily become a nuisance. Since January 2026 Suno has a Sounds mode that makes
single short sound effects, on the Pro plan. Background: the sound list
([`docs/design/sound.md`](../design/sound.md)).

## Decision

- The game has 17 sound effects. In a level, only a dot landing, a capture, danger to the player's
  own group, the dots running low, the boss's special turn and the boosters make a sound. Buttons,
  notes and cards are silent.
- Three effects (the dot, the capture ring, the bomb) start from a source sound made with Suno
  Sounds during the Suno Pro month of decision 0013, under the same prompt rules as the music. If
  Suno's version is not clean, or its terms do not cover Sounds, the owner records that sound with
  a phone.
- The other 14 effects are notes made in code by Claude. CC0 sounds stay a last resort.

## Consequences

- The recording session shrinks from 21 sounds to none, or at most three.
- Each Suno sound gets a register row with its prompt and link, like a music track.
- The sound effects add about 0.1 MB to the app; the music is what decides the audio's size.
