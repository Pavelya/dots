# 0015: Launch with about 75 levels in 4 worlds, plus a daily puzzle

**Date:** 2026-11-05 · **Status:** accepted

## Context

Puzzle games launch with enough levels to hold players for weeks and then add worlds regularly. Background: the design investigation (https://claude.ai/artifact/DyiSkrrx3hwARfRpyxNGRP).

## Decision

- About 75 levels in 4 worlds at launch: Notebook, Garden, Night sky, Sea.
- A daily puzzle, the same for everyone.
- A new world every 1 to 2 months after launch.

## Consequences

- Every level is checked by the puzzle solver and the difficulty bench before it ships (plan tasks G-03, G-11).
