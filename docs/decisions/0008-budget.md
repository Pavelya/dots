# 0008: Budget: free tools, the Play account and one month of paid music

**Date:** 2026-11-05 · **Status:** accepted

## Context

The project aims at free tools and free tiers. Background: the design investigation ([`docs/design/investigation.md`](../design/investigation.md)).

## Decision

- Free tools and free tiers wherever possible.
- Two paid items are accepted: the one-time $25 Google Play developer account, and one month of a paid Suno plan to make music with commercial rights.

## Consequences

- Anything else that costs money needs its own decision record.
