# 0013: Music from one Suno Pro month; sound effects made by us

**Date:** 2026-11-05 · **Status:** accepted

## Context

Songs made on Suno's free plan stay Suno's and are non-commercial; songs made while on a paid plan carry commercial rights that last after cancelling (per Suno's help pages as seen in search results; to be checked before subscribing). Background: the design investigation (https://claude.ai/artifact/DyiSkrrx3hwARfRpyxNGRP).

## Decision

- Music: subscribe to Suno Pro for the month the music is made, keep every prompt and output, then cancel.
- Sound effects: recorded or generated by us, plus CC0 sounds where needed.

## Consequences

- Each track is checked with a song-recognition app before use (plan task V-09).
