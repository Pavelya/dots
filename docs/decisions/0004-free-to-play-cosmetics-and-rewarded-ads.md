# 0004: Free to play: cosmetics and optional rewarded ads

**Date:** 2026-11-05 · **Status:** accepted

## Context

The game needs a way to earn money that does not spoil a skill game. Paid advantages are a common complaint in reviews, and paid random items (loot boxes) bring extra Google Play rules. Background: the design investigation (https://claude.ai/artifact/DyiSkrrx3hwARfRpyxNGRP).

## Decision

- The game is free to download and play.
- It sells cosmetics only: themes, piece styles, ring effects.
- Rewarded ads are optional: the player chooses to watch one for a small reward. No forced ads.
- Nothing for sale changes the rules or the odds of winning; no random paid items.

## Consequences

- Every reward that can come from an ad can also be earned by playing.
- Ads need the EU and UK consent message and are declared in Data safety (plan tasks C-01 to C-04, R-03).
