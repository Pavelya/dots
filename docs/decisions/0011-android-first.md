# 0011: Android first, assets ready for iOS

**Date:** 2026-11-05 · **Status:** accepted

## Context

The POC is an Android app; iOS may come later. Background: the design investigation ([`docs/design/investigation.md`](../design/investigation.md)).

## Decision

- Android is the only platform for the first release.
- Art and sound are made in formats and sizes that also work for iOS.

## Consequences

- The engine stays free of Android code (already the case).
