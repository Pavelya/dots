# 0002: POC app ID, test distribution and testers

**Date:** 2026-11-04 · **Status:** accepted

- **App ID:** `io.github.pavelya.dots`. It cannot change once the app is published, so the
  full version keeps it.
- **Distribution for the test:** a debug-signed APK attached to a GitHub Release; testers allow
  the install once. No Play Console account is needed for the POC.
- **Testers:** 5 to 10 people with Android phones, at least 2 who played Dots on paper and at
  least 2 who never heard of it.
