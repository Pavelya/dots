# 0009: Asset ownership: clean, recorded sources for everything

**Date:** 2026-11-05 · **Status:** accepted

## Context

AI-generated art may not be protected by copyright, so others could copy it. The owner accepts that. What must never happen is a claim that we used someone else's work. Background: the design investigation (https://claude.ai/artifact/DyiSkrrx3hwARfRpyxNGRP).

## Decision

- Every image, sound, music track and font comes from a clean source: made by us, generated by us under the prompt rules, CC0, or a licence that allows commercial use in an app.
- Every asset gets a row in the asset register (docs/assets.md) with its tool, plan, date, prompt or source link and licence.
- Prompts never name artists, studios, games or brands; images we do not own are never uploaded as references.

## Consequences

- A CI check rejects asset files without a register row (plan task V-01).
- Key art (logo, icon, main backgrounds) is reverse-image-searched and finished by a person.
