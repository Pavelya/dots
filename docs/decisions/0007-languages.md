# 0007: Languages: English first, then Russian, Ukrainian and Polish

**Date:** 2026-11-05 · **Status:** accepted

## Context

The paper game is known in Russia, Ukraine, Poland and Czechia and little known in the West. Background: the design investigation ([`docs/design/investigation.md`](../design/investigation.md)).

## Decision

- English first.
- Russian, Ukrainian and Polish next.

## Consequences

- All text stays in resource files with proper plural forms (already the case in the POC).
- Each translation is checked by a fluent speaker (plan task A-10).
