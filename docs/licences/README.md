day it was saved (`name-YYYY-MM-DD`), and the register's Licence column links to it.

**The Suno help pages** speak only of songs. The Terms of Service are what count, and the help
pages say so themselves.

## Suno: Sounds as well as songs

Three sound effects come from Suno Sounds, a beta mode for short sounds. The Terms of Service
cover them as they cover songs:

- **Output is any audio.** The terms call everything the service makes "Output": "audio and visual
  output". Nothing in them limits it to songs, and Suno publishes no separate terms for Sounds.
- **Pro owns its output.** "Pro and Premier Accounts": Suno "assigns to you all of its right,
  title and interest in and to any Output" made while subscribed to Pro or Premier.
- **Commercial use needs a download.** "Permitted Commercial Use": Output may be used commercially
  if it was downloaded through Suno's own download, within the plan's download allowance. Ripping
  or recording it is not allowed. These rights last after the subscription ends.
- **Editing is allowed.** The terms allow editing, processing and converting the format. Our
  scripts cut, filter and convert the files. Suno may add a watermark or metadata, which may not be
  removed to hide where a file came from; the register names Suno openly for every one of these
  files.
- **No copyright promise.** Suno does not promise that copyright exists in any Output. The
  assignment and the commercial-use rights hold either way.

So the dot, the ring and the bomb sounds can stay, on two conditions the owner confirms: they were
made on 2026-11-09 while subscribed to Pro, and they were saved with Suno's download button. The
same holds for the six music tracks.
